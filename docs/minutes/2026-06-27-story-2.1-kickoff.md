**Present:** the engineer, Priya (PO).
**Topic:** scope of Epic 2 search; whether to bring in a search engine.

## Notes

- Priya: users mostly want to re-find a link they saved, by a word in the title or a tag. Not building Google.
- Engineer: two realistic options — Postgres full-text search, or stand up Elasticsearch.
  - Postgres FTS: no new infra, always consistent with writes, ranks fine for this size. No typo tolerance.
  - Elasticsearch: better relevance and fuzzy matching, but a whole service to run/secure/sync for a per-user dataset of thousands of rows.
- Priya: typo tolerance would be nice-to-have, not launch-critical. Agreed to defer.
- Discussed ranking: title matches should beat description-only matches. Engineer: Postgres `setweight` does this in the index.
- Cross-user / public search? Priya: not now — that's the deferred Sharing epic.

## Decisions

- **Use Postgres full-text search for Story 2.1.** Engineer to write it up as an ADR (→ became [ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-postgres-fts-over-elasticsearch.md)).
- **Typo tolerance deferred** — recorded as a risk in the plan and a revisit trigger in the ADR.
- **Search is per-user only** — tenant isolation is a hard requirement (AC5).
